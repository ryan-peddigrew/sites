Questions

Good to know.

Who is PdR for?

Built for event planners and event hosts who believe the right restaurant sets the tone for the entire experience. PdR was created by Samantha Mele after more than 15 years sourcing private dining experiences for clients including Web Summit, CoinDesk, Hyatt, TD, Pinterest, and many more. We built the platform we always wished existed.

What does it cost?

Restaurants pay nothing until a planner actually books with them — list your rooms and receive qualified inquiries for free. Hosts and planners can start free with core search, with a membership tier that unlocks advanced filters, the concierge and preferred replies. Founding-member pricing is shared during onboarding.

When does it launch?

We are onboarding founding members now, in waves, ahead of the public launch. Request early access to hold your place in the next wave.

Is my information safe?

Early-access details are used only to contact you about onboarding — never sold or shared. You can ask us to remove your information at any time.
