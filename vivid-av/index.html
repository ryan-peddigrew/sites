Questions

Before you
book.

Straight answers, sorted by where you are in the plan.

Do you do both events and permanent installs?

Yes. Events and installs come from one partner, so you don't need to juggle multiple vendors.

Where do you work?

Across Toronto and the GTA.

How do we start?

Tell us about your event or your space, by quote request or a complimentary discovery call. Vivid designs an AV plan around your goals, space and budget.

What kinds of events do you support?

Meetings, conferences and corporate events of any size, including hybrid events with live streaming.

Will someone be on site?

Yes. Experienced technicians make sure everything runs smoothly from start to finish.

Can remote guests watch live?

Yes. Hybrid and live streaming connect your in-person and remote audiences.

Will our team be able to use it?

That's the point. Systems are designed for ease of use, so there's no tech confusion on meeting day.

Does it work with Zoom and Teams?

Yes. Video conferencing setups are Zoom and Teams ready.

What happens after the install?

Ongoing support is available, so your rooms keep working long after day one.
