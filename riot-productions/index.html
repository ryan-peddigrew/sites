Culture change that sticks

Sandra has taken part in culture change from both sides: winning the work and delivering it in the room. She helps leaders define the culture they want, then build the habits that hold it in place.

    Good to know

    Who does Sandra work with?

    Business owners and leadership teams, from small and mid-sized businesses to large organizations. Hotels, events and hospitality are a long-time home.

    What is experiential learning?

    Learning by doing. Teams take on a real challenge together, then talk through what happened and why. People remember what they lived through far longer than what they read on a slide.

    Where do sessions happen?

    At your place, somewhere new, or online. Sandra is based in Toronto and has worked in more than 70 countries.

    How long is a program?

    Anything from a single conference session or half day to a program that runs over several weeks with coaching in between.

    How is the work priced?

    Every engagement is built around your goals and your group. After a first conversation, Sandra sends a clear proposal.
