About

Courtney Stanley is recognized globally as an award-winning changemaker, compelling keynote speaker, energetic event emcee and creator of the podcast Dare to Interrupt.

Through her leadership and use of voice, she has earned seats on international boards of directors as the youngest member ever inducted. She helps women, young professionals, entrepreneurs and organizations have the conversations that change teams, culture and careers.

She is most proud of her work to prevent sexual harassment and assault through #MeetingsToo, an events-centred social movement she launched.
