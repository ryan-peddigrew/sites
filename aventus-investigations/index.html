STRAIGHT ANSWERS

WHAT PEOPLE ASK FIRST.

The questions most people are afraid to ask out loud.

Is hiring a private investigator legal?

Yes. Aventus is a licensed Ontario agency (licence 11457769) and every investigation stays within the law and a code of ethics.

Will the person find out?

Surveillance is planned around the person's routine and carried out to stay unnoticed, with no confrontation. Your consultation and file stay confidential.

Can you track a phone or get into accounts?

No. We only use lawful methods: surveillance, public records, authorized databases and open-source research. Anyone who offers more is breaking the law.

What do I actually get?

A clear chronological report with dates, times, locations, observations, and photos or video where appropriate. Facts, not assumptions.

How is the price set?

Every case is different, so most work is quoted after a free, confidential consultation. Set-scope work, like locates and background checks, can be booked from the rate card.

How fast can you start?

We're open 24/7, 365 days a year, and reply to every request within one business day. Tell us if it's urgent.
