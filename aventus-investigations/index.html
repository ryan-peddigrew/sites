HOW A CASE RUNS

FROM FIRST CALL TO FINAL REPORT.

Most people have never hired an investigator. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. 01

    TALK IT THROUGH

    A free, confidential consultation. We discuss your concerns, your goals and what you already know.

  2. 02

    A PLAN FOR YOUR CASE

    No two cases are the same. We build the approach around the people, places, schedules and objectives involved.

  3. 03

    QUIET FIELDWORK

    Lawful surveillance and research, planned to stay unnoticed. Every time, place and observation is recorded as it happens.

  4. 04

    A CLEAR REPORT

    A chronological report with dates, times, locations, and photos or video where appropriate. Facts, not assumptions, ready for you or your lawyer.
