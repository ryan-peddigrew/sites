      STRAIGHT ANSWERS

      GOOD QUESTIONS.

      How is Gillis different from a traditional outsourced sales company?

      We don't simply assign a salesperson to your hotel. We bring the leadership, systems, coaching, accountability, technology and proven processes that build repeatable sales performance, as an extension of your team.

      How does remote hotel sales work?

      Your seller works remotely but becomes fully part of your hotel: they represent your property, work with your General Manager and Revenue Manager, use your systems, join regular meetings and focus on proactive selling.

      Will my customers know they're working with Gillis?

      No. Your dedicated seller represents your hotel as part of your team, aligned with your brand and your messaging.

      Can Gillis replace our on-property Director of Sales?

      For many select-service and extended-stay hotels, yes. Many brands recognize an approved third-party sales partner as an alternative. Requirements vary, so confirm with your brand representative.

      How quickly can you begin selling?

      Every partnership starts with a structured two-week onboarding. We take on a select number of new hotels each month so every client gets full attention.

      What happens if my salesperson is away?

      Your hotel is supported by a whole sales organization, not one person. Our structure keeps service running without a gap.

      Do you use AI in your sales process?

      Yes. Every managed sales engagement is supported by GillisOne, our proprietary sales intelligence and AI platform designed specifically for hotel sales. Rather than replacing the human element of sales, GillisOne enhances it.

      Does Gillis Sales work if we already have a sales team?

      Absolutely. Many clients use us to extend prospecting, add leadership, support new initiatives or strengthen execution.
