[kwel] : verb - to put an end to, to subdue or silence.

We create opportunities for our talent, our clients, our future.

Quell represents diverse chefs, certified drink experts, and health and lifestyle and food and drink content creators: accredited leaders in their fields, with strong followings and visible industry presence.

Disrupt the system to make meaningful and real change. The faces of the food, drink, health and lifestyle industry should better reflect the diversity of society. Systemic barriers limit inclusion and opportunity for exceptional talent, and skew how food and drink are shown. Quell is committed to pushing the industry forward, and asks for your allyship.
