Our services

Eight ways
to own the room.

The right solutions to fit any budget, anywhere in North America. From concept design to on-site delivery, we work closely with you to bring your ideas to life.

  1. Make your message stand out. We partner with meeting planners and blend audiovisual technology with set design, lighting, visuals and audio, so your vision takes centre stage and resonates long after the event ends.

  2. For over 20 years we have led the way in LED, turning any venue into a spectacular environment. From concept to installation, with precision engineering and dedicated on-site support.

  3. LED lighting in millions of vibrant colours turns an ordinary room into something extraordinary. Our lighting designers help you choose the right look, eco-friendly and economical.

  4. Professional audio is the foundation of any event: clarity, no feedback, no echoes, so the audience stays on the message. Great visuals attract attention; great audio reinforces it.

  5. Corporate events are business theatre. Stock or fully custom set pieces transform a typical space into a vibrant centre of attention, however big or small, simple or complex.

  6. Turn any surface into a canvas. Using our Pandora's Box media servers, we project onto flat or irregular surfaces. Limited only by your imagination.

  7. Extend your event beyond the walls. Real-time, high-definition coverage optimized for clear audio and seamless streaming across platforms, with live audience engagement tools.

  8. Communicate with impact and cut printed waste. Touchscreens, digital podiums and banners, updated instantly across multi-day events.
