Purpose

About this guide

This guide shows how the existing Center Stage AV logo is used, and gives everyone who makes material for the company one set of rules.

It covers the logo, colours, type, imagery, buttons, and the everyday pieces: business cards, letterhead, letters, email signatures and the newsletter.

Keep to the rules here. Where a case is not covered, ask before making something new.

Company

Who we are

Center Stage AV plans events, produces them on site, and rents audiovisual equipment.

Plan

Concept, floor plans, rigging and show flow, from the boardroom to the ballroom.

Produce

Staging, lighting, sound, video and screens, installed and run by our own crew.

Rent

Audiovisual equipment for events of every size, delivered and supported.

Name

Our name

Write the name the same way every time:

Correct

Center Stage AV

CENTER STAGE AV (in headlines and the logo only)

Not correct

Centre Stage AV, Center Stage A/V, CenterStage AV, Center Stage Audio Visual

Spell "Center" the American way, as the logo does, even in Canadian copy.

07 / 30
Logo

The logo

The logo has two parts: the wordmark CENTER STAGE and the AV emblem, a crimson oval with a white AV. Use the logo exactly as supplied. Never redraw it.

Their logo as supplied (black type on white) and the white-type version for dark backgrounds.

08 / 30
Construction

Clear space and construction

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter A in CENTER on every side. Nothing enters this space: no text, no photo edge, no other logo.

09 / 30
Size

Minimum size

Print

Logo at least 30 mm wide. The wordmark must stay readable at that size.

Screen

Logo at least 140 px wide on desktop and 110 px on phones. Use the largest file you have, and never stretch it.

The supplied artwork is 297 px wide. Ask for a vector or a larger original before printing banners or signage.

12 / 30
Emblem

The AV emblem alone

Where space is tight, such as a favicon, app icon, social avatar or a stage screen corner, use the AV emblem alone. Crop it from the full logo; do not redraw it.

Current status: the emblem crop for these uses is still to be made from the master artwork.

Contrast and balance

19 / 30
Type

Capitals rules

Use capitals for headlines, sub titles, menus and buttons. Keep body copy in sentence case. Never use italics on the brand type.

Correct

Build your quote

We can help you plan the whole event.

Not correct

Build your quote in italics

Iconography

Icons are line icons at about 40 px, full size. They sit on their own, with no box or square around them.

22 / 30
Layout

Grid and spacing

Use a 12-column grid on desktop and a single column on phones with a 16 px side gutter.

Space in steps of 8 px: 8, 16, 24, 40, 64.

Tap targets are at least 44 px tall. Nothing sits closer than 8 px to another target.

23 / 30
Buttons

Buttons and calls to action

Build your quote Get in touch

Primary action is a Crimson button with white caps. Secondary is an outline. Put the two side by side. Never show a phone number on a button.

24 / 30
Voice

Voice and tone

Say it like this

"We will have the stage built and tested before doors open."

Not like this

"Our solutions leverage best-in-class synergies for your event."

Plain English, confident, specific. Short sentences. No jargon, no hype.
