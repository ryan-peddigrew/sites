      Exclusive Affair Rental

      After years in the event industry, Mike and Edie Devine found Exclusive Affair Rental. It becomes a cornerstone of the event rental market.

    2. Growth

      Exclusive Display

      The family expands into the trade show and exhibit space.

      Specialty furniture for events and trade shows joins the family of companies.

    4. Today

      Divine Furniture Rental

      After a brief retirement, Mike Devine returns with a renewed vision. Divine specializes in cutting-edge event and trade show furnishings, backed by a team of logistics experts, carpenters, painters, seamstresses, upholsterers, truck drivers and onsite specialists. Proudly family owned and operated.

    Sustainability

    Built to
    last longer.

    We prioritize sustainability in everything we do, and we're always open to learning. Have an idea we could put to work? We'd love to hear it.

    Food-safe cleaning

    All cleaning products used on our inventory are environmentally friendly and food-safe.

    Reusable covers

    The first rental company in Canada to use reusable, custom-made covers for every product, made from recycled plastic.

    Plastic reuse

    Where wrapping is needed for safe delivery, we reuse it wherever possible and recycle the rest at our facility.

    No plastic bottles

    A filtered water system lets our warehouse team refill company-provided reusable bottles.

    Repaired in-house

    Our carpentry and upholstery teams repair pieces in-house, extending their life and keeping them out of landfill.

    GPS truck routing

    Every route is planned by GPS to run efficiently and reduce emissions.

    Donated, not discarded

    When pieces are retired, we donate them to Habitat for Humanity, a partnership we cherish.
