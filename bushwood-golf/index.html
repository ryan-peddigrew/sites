Markham, Ontario

An eighteen, a nine, and a patio that keeps you after the round.

Nestled in northeastern Markham, about three miles south of Stouffville, Bushwood is made for good company: two public courses, a bent grass range, and the Caddy Shack Lounge overlooking the ninth.

We look after the land properly, with computerized irrigation, wildflower plantings, Purple Martin birdhouses, and a ball mark repair program, so the greens, tees and fairways are worth the trip.

Booking a tee time

Book online any time, or call the pro shop at 905-640-1233 and press 1. Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead; unused or no-show tee times are charged to the booking party.

Rates

Rates vary with demand, season and promotions, so the live price shows when you book. Prices are subject to change and HST.

Waiver

Every golfer signs a waiver, which takes a minute online.

Carts and walking

Cart renters must be 21 or older, hold a valid driver's licence and sign a cart waiver in the pro shop. Walkers, when permitted, pay a walker's fee, stay with their group and must be 10 or older.

Pace of play

Most Championship rounds finish in under four hours, and the front nine takes 20 to 30 minutes longer than the back. The Executive takes 90 to 110 minutes. No fivesomes, and each golfer needs their own clubs; rentals are available in the pro shop.

Age limits

Executive course: 8 and up with an adult. Championship course: 12 and up with an adult.

Rain cheques

Issued after a torrential downpour. Wait 30 minutes at the pro shop with your original receipt. Valid only in the season issued, with no cash value.

Dress code

Golf pants, shorts, skirts and skorts. Collared shirts preferred; mock necks and T-shirts are fine, as are ladies' sleeveless golf shirts. No men's tank tops, sleeveless T-shirts, spaghetti straps, track or fleece pants. Denim discouraged. Soft spikes or soft soles.

Getting here

From Hwy 401 or 407, east to Hwy 48 (Markham Rd) north, right on Elgin Mills Rd E, right on Reesor Rd. From the north, Hwy 48 south, left on Elgin Mills, right on Reesor.
