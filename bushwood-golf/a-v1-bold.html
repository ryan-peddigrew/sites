Golf the way it should feel.

Nestled in northeastern Markham, about three miles south of Stouffville, Bushwood is two public courses, a bent grass range, and the Caddy Shack Lounge, all on one property.

We look after it properly: computerized irrigation, wildflower plantings, Purple Martin birdhouses, a ball mark repair program, and real attention on the greens, tee decks and fairways.

How do I book a tee time?

Online any time, or call the pro shop at 905-640-1233 and press 1. Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead; unused or no-show tee times are charged to the booking party.

What are your rates?

Rates change with demand, season and promotions, so the live price shows when you book. Prices are subject to change and HST.

Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes, every golfer signs one. It takes a minute online.

Power carts and walking

Cart renters must be 21+, hold a valid driver's licence and sign a cart waiver in the pro shop. Walkers, when allowed, pay a walker's fee, stay with their group and must be 10 or older.

How long is a round?

Most Championship rounds are under four hours; the front nine takes 20 to 30 minutes longer than the back. The Executive course takes 90 to 110 minutes. No fivesomes, and every golfer needs their own clubs (rentals in the pro shop).

Age limits

Executive course: 8+ with an adult. Championship course: 12+ with an adult.

Rain cheques

Issued after a torrential downpour. Wait 30 minutes at the pro shop with your original receipt. Valid only in the season issued, no cash value.

Dress code

Golf pants, shorts, skirts and skorts. Collared shirts preferred; mock necks and T-shirts are fine, as are ladies' sleeveless golf shirts. No men's tank tops, sleeveless T-shirts, spaghetti straps, track or fleece pants. Denim discouraged. Soft spikes or soft soles.

Directions

From Hwy 401 or 407: east to Hwy 48 (Markham Rd) north, right on Elgin Mills Rd E, right on Reesor Rd. From the north: Hwy 48 south, left on Elgin Mills, right on Reesor.
