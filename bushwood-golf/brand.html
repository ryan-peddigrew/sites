Clear space and size

Keep a margin around the logo equal to the height of the golf ball in Gordy's arm. Never smaller than 90px tall on screen or 25mm in print; below that, use Gordy alone.

04

Gordy the Gopher

The gopher now has a name and a job. He is never redrawn: he is dressed with simple props for each part of the business, and he hosts the website chat.

Tournament GordyTrophy. Tournaments and outings.
"I do" GordyTop hat and bow tie. Weddings.
Patio GordyA cold one. The lounge and celebrations.
League GordyPin flag. Leagues and the chat.
Voice. Friendly, quick, a bit cheeky. Speaks in the first person: "I live here, so ask me anything."
Props only. Add an object beside or on him. Never change his face, fur or pose.
One Gordy per view. He is the punchline, not the wallpaper.
05

Photography

Bushwood already owns strong photography. Lead with it: golden hour, people enjoying themselves, the course from above. No stock.

Do. Warm evening light, real golfers and staff, wide aerials, the patio.
Avoid. Flat midday shots, empty rooms, heavy filters, text over faces.
Crop. Let photos run edge to edge. Square corners.

07

Voice

Bushwood already sounds like itself. Keep the wit, tidy the delivery.

Keep saying
  • "Not just another golf course. We're Bushwood."
  • "Talk to the caddy."
  • "Fore! The reviews are in."
  • "Good times. Great golf."
How to write it
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Sentence case, Canadian spelling.
  • Say "book", "reserve" and "enquire".
  • Facts over superlatives: par, yards, minutes, prices.
