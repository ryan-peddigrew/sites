How it works

Follow one bag from the top floor to the truck

Every part of that trip is something we build, install, clean or service. Scroll to follow it.

01 / 07 · Chute door

A resident drops a bag down the chute

Every floor has a chute door that has to close and latch on its own. A door that sticks open lets odour and fire travel. We inspect every chute door, floor by floor.

Garbage & linen chutes
02 / 07 · The chute

It falls past every floor

Residue builds up on the chute walls and causes odour, bacteria and a fire risk. We degrease and disinfect chutes with enzyme cleaners and biodegradable degreaser, and check the washdown system.

Chute & compactor cleaning
03 / 07 · Fire protection

Dampers and fire-rated doors hold the line

Fire-rated doors and dampers stop fire and smoke from moving between floors through the chute. We replace liners, doors and dampers, up to a full floor-by-floor retrofit.

Garbage & linen chutes
04 / 07 · Tri-sorter

The sorter sends it to the right bin

The resident picks garbage, recycling or organics at the chute door, and the sorter turns to the matching bin. Recycling becomes as easy as garbage. We build each sorter to fit the room that's already there.

Tri-sorter & bi-sorter retrofits
05 / 07 · Compactor

The compactor packs it down

A hydraulic ram packs the waste so the building needs fewer pickups. Grease on the moving parts, clean hydraulic oil and sound belts keep it running. Skip them and it fails at the worst time.

Compactor manufacturing & service
06 / 07 · Odour control

The air gets cleaned around the clock

Garbage room odour travels up the chute to the floors where residents live. A fan pulls the room's air through UV light and a HEPA filter, which destroys odours instead of covering them up. CSA approved.

Odour control
07 / 07 · Out to the truck

Bins roll out for pickup

Front-end-load containers built for the room, with wheels, casters and bottoms we repair. Battery bin movers let staff move heavy containers without getting hurt.

FEL containers & bins
Cleaning, before and after

Same room. New floor.

We degrease and disinfect chutes, compactors and garbage rooms with biodegradable products, and can refinish the floor with epoxy so it stays clean.

    Six warning signs to spot on a walk-through

    Catch these early and a service call stays a service call, not an emergency.

    A chute door won't close on its own

    We check: every chute door, floor by floor.

    Residents notice a smell in the halls

    We check: the odour control unit and its filters, and clean the chute.

    The compactor is slow, loud or leaking oil

    We check: the hydraulic oil, belts and grease.

    Bins are hard to roll, rusted or leaking

    We check: for holes, rust, and worn wheels and casters.

    The chute washdown doesn't run

    We check: the washdown system.

    A dirty garbage room, or pests

    We check: and clean the room, bins, chute and compactor.
