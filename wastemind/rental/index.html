Rental plans

Compactor and Baler Rental

Full-maintenance rentals for compactors and balers: predictable monthly cost, current equipment, and repairs handled by WasteMind.

Why rent

Why rent on a full-maintenance plan?

Renting turns a large capital purchase into a fixed operating cost, and puts equipment repairs on us. Every full-maintenance rental plan includes:

  • No capital required
  • Latest equipment and technology
  • Guaranteed fixed monthly rate
  • Accurate cost budgeting
  • Repairs and maintenance included

What you can rent

Four ways to put it on a plan

Self-Contained Compactor Rental

Compactor and container in one sealed unit, the standard choice for wet waste and odour-sensitive sites.

Stationary Compactor Rental

High-volume compaction into a detachable receiver container.

Vertical Baler Rental

Bale cardboard and recyclables to cut hauling costs and free up space.

LoPro Compactor Rental

Low-profile compactor for garbage rooms and loading areas with limited height.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What coverage is included in repairs and services?

Full-maintenance rentals cover equipment repairs, with exceptions for damage resulting from negligence or improper operation, including damage caused during transportation.

What types of equipment are available for rent?

Stationary compactors, self-contained compactors, LoPro compactors, vertical balers and high-rise residential compactors, available new or certified pre-owned.

Can the equipment be customized to our building?

Yes. Our full production shop lets us fabricate custom installations designed to your garbage room and your requirements.

What is the rental term?

We offer standard terms and can customize the term length to suit your building. Ask us about the options when you request a quote.

Can we change the plan or buy the equipment later?

Absolutely. We work with you on a plan that fits your requirements, with the flexibility to modify it or initiate an equipment buyout.

Do you cater to multiple sites or only single rentals?

Both. For organizations with multiple sites we create customized rental plans to suit the needs of each location.

Ready to price a rental?

Tell us about your building and waste volumes and we'll recommend the right unit and plan.
