Local manufacturing

Manufactured in Toronto, serviced across the GTA

Because our fabrication shop is in Toronto, GTA buildings get equipment built to their garbage room by the same team that installs and services it. From tri-sorter retrofits and chute door replacements to compactor repairs and odour control, our representatives coordinate the work and our technicians deliver it.

FAQ

Toronto & GTA service questions

Do you have a Toronto office?

Our Toronto office is coming soon. Our manufacturing facility is already in Toronto, and our GTA East and GTA West representatives serve customers across the region today.

Which parts of the GTA do you cover?

Both sides of the city. Zach Burke-Fagan handles GTA West and Peter Pettit handles GTA East and Toronto. We also serve the Niagara region and communities between Toronto and Ottawa.

Do you service North York, East York, Markham and Mississauga?

Yes. Our GTA East and GTA West representatives cover the whole Greater Toronto Area, including North York, East York, Markham and Mississauga, for compactor repair, garbage chute repair and cleaning, and odour control.

Is your equipment made locally?

Yes. Our compactors and tri-sorter and bi-sorter systems are manufactured in our Toronto facility by fabricators with over 25 years of experience.

Need a quote, a service plan or an emergency repair?

Talk to a WasteMind specialist in Ottawa or the GTA. We offer 24-hour service and always provide a solution.
