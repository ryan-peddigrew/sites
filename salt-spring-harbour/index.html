Salt Spring Island, British Columbia

Eight facilities.
Five harbours.

HASSI runs eight facilities in five harbours: Ganges, Fulford, Vesuvius, Burgoyne Bay and Musgrave Landing. Transient moorage is offered at all harbours, to varying degrees.

The Harbour Manager office is at Centennial Dock in Ganges. It is open daily in business hours, except Sundays in the winter season.

Dock directory

Pick a harbour first

Tap a dock to open its details. Ganges has the full set of services. The outer harbours are simpler: honour pay boxes, a wharfinger who visits, and help from the Harbour Office when you need it.

Ganges - Centennial

The largest HASSI facility. It is mainly homeport for fishing, commercial and recreational vessels with permanent contracts.

No transient tourist use without staff approval first.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp shore power
  • Lighting
  • Garbage disposal
  • Waste oil disposal
  • Shower and washroom facilities
  • Boat launching ramp
Ganges - Kanaka

Mainly for daily transient vessels from May 1 to September 30.

From October 1 to April 30, some longer-term space may open, with a waiting list. The south side stays open for daily transient use all year.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp shore power
  • Lighting
  • Garbage, waste oil, showers, washrooms and ramp through Centennial
Ganges - Breakwater

Former transport facility, in HASSI care since May 1, 1998. Commercial use, with the Coast Guard building, reserved slips and parking.

The float is used for loading and offloading, and by larger transient commercial vessels. No monthly moorage without special Board approval.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp shore power on the centre finger only
  • Lighting
  • Other services through Centennial
  • Black water pump-out station here

Two outer finger floats are reserved for float planes. The innermost is for RCMP and Coast Guard use.

Vesuvius Bay

On the northwest side of the island, just north of the Vesuvius ferry terminal. Mainly transient boaters, and busy in summer.

  • Honour pay box with envelopes
  • Wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • Emergency power through the Harbour Office
  • Night lighting, bus service and parking

A licensed restaurant with take-out and a coffee shop are within walking distance. Short hiking trails start from the parking lot.

Burgoyne Bay

On the west side, at the north end of Sansum Narrows across from Maple Bay. The small bay is sheltered from south and east wind and waves.

  • No services
  • Some parking and road access
  • Honour pay box, wharfinger visits
  • Solar beacon marks the float end at night

Half the float is reserved for the Burgoyne Bay Residents Association, marked by a yellow tie-up rail. The other half is short-term transient use, up to 48 hours.

Musgrave Landing

On the west side, at the south end of Sansum Narrows across from Cowichan Bay. Mainly transient boaters, and busy in summer.

  • Honour pay box, wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • A few parking spots and road access

The road is unsurfaced and can be hard for low-clearance vehicles in bad weather.

Fulford Outer

Just south of the ferry terminal in downtown Fulford. A transient-use facility.

  • Honour pay box, wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • Emergency power through the Harbour Office
  • Washrooms usually on the adjoining ferry dock in working hours
  • Bus service
  • Night lighting

There is a marked area for water ambulance, loading and water taxis. A public derrick with an electric winch sits on the wharfhead. It is locked, and access is through the Harbour Office.

Fulford Inner

Just north of the ferry terminal. Homeport for fishing and recreation boats.

  • Potable water, isolated in winter
  • Lighting and shore power
  • No garbage facilities

A waiting list for long-term assignments is kept at the Harbour Office. HASSI policy does not allow liveaboards here. There is no room for transient users.

In Ganges, 30-amp power, water, Wi-Fi, shower facilities and garbage disposal are offered for customers paying for moorage on vessels over 12 feet in length.

Transient moorage is limited to 72 hours, except where the Harbour Manager authorizes more. Check-out is 10:00 AM the next morning.

From 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, two hours of moorage are free. After 2 hours, half the daily rate applies. After 4:00 PM, the full daily rate applies.

Ganges is a zero-discharge harbour. Vessels with holding tanks are asked to use the pump-out station at the Breakwater Float. Water is free for vessels paying for moorage, with no wash-downs. Water is turned off in winter to prevent freezing.

Monthly and quarterly moorage are offered only at Centennial year round, Fulford Inner year round, and Kanaka from October 1 to April 30.

Centennial reserve fees: dinghy float $61.90 per month plus tax; small boat floats $21.00 per month plus tax, charged quarterly, plus the moorage fee; first float commercial $525.00 per year plus tax, plus the moorage fee.

Power: 30-amp connections are at Centennial, Kanaka and the Breakwater. Proper twist-lock marine connectors are required. No pig tails are permitted.

Long-term moorage

Join the waitlist

Applications are being accepted for long-term moorage. Numbers on file already cover more than 5 years at the current turnover rate. Salt Spring Island residents get top priority.

The list is long and slow moving. If you have an application on file, check in from time to time to keep it up to date.

Fish sales

Fresh from the dock

The Harbour Authority manages a fish sales dock at the main Centennial Dock, by the harbour office at 127 Fulford-Ganges Road.

Commercial fishers use this dock to sell direct to the public. Products have included shrimp, tuna, salmon and crab.

As much advance notice as possible is given, and signage is posted when sales occur.

News

Notices for boaters

Winter moorage and hours

Winter moorage runs October 1 to May 1 inside Kanaka Dock. Office hours change to 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on October 1, and the office is closed on Sundays in winter.

Rates now in effect

Transient moorage is $1.60 per foot per night on all Salt Spring Island docks. 30-amp power is $10.00 per night. See Rates for full pricing.

Pump-out and used oil

The pump-out is open after repairs. Used motor oil is accepted from vessels only, and containers are not accepted.

Membership

Have a say in the harbours

To take part in running the Harbour Authority of Salt Spring Island, membership is required in the Harbour Authority of Salt Spring Island Association.

Membership is approved by the Board of Directors. Membership applications are available at the main harbour office.

Members pages on the current site cover Members News, Audit Reports and Minutes.

Harbour Authority of Salt Spring Island

Eight facilities in five harbours on Salt Spring Island, British Columbia, operated by the Harbour Authority of Salt Spring Island.

Do not use this website for navigation.

HASSI was incorporated by Letters Patent on May 23, 1990. It is a not-for-profit body corporate under Canada's Corporation Act. Its purpose is to operate, maintain and manage public fishing harbours, and to provide facilities and services to community and marine stakeholders.
