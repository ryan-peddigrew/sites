01 · Harbours

Pick a harbour.

Ganges has the full set of services. The outer harbours are simpler: honour pay boxes, a wharfinger who visits, and help from the Harbour Office when you need it.

01Ganges3 docks

Centennial

The largest HASSI facility. Mainly homeport for fishing, commercial and recreational vessels with permanent contracts.

No transient use without staff approval first.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp power
  • Lighting
  • Garbage
  • Waste oil
  • Showers and washrooms
  • Boat ramp

Kanaka

Mainly for daily transient vessels from May 1 to September 30. From October 1 to April 30, some longer-term space may open, with a waiting list. The outside of the Kanaka header float stays open for transient moorage.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp power
  • Lighting
  • Other services through Centennial

Breakwater

Former transport dock, in HASSI care since 1998. Commercial use, with the Coast Guard building, reserved slips and parking. The float is used for loading, offloading and larger transient commercial vessels.

Black water pump-out station here. Two outer finger floats are reserved for float planes; the innermost is for RCMP and Coast Guard use.

  • Potable water
  • Wi-Fi
  • 30-amp power, centre finger
  • Lighting
  • Pump-out
02Fulford2 docks

Fulford Outer

Just south of the ferry terminal in downtown Fulford. A transient-use facility, with a marked area for water ambulance, loading and water taxis.

The public derrick with an electric winch is locked. Access is through the Harbour Office.

  • Honour pay box
  • Wharfinger visits
  • Emergency power via office
  • Bus service
  • Night lighting

Fulford Inner

Just north of the ferry terminal. Homeport for fishing and recreation boats. A waiting list for long-term assignments is kept at the Harbour Office.

No liveaboards. No room for transient users.

  • Potable water, off in winter
  • Lighting
  • Shore power
  • No garbage
03Vesuvius1 dock

Vesuvius Bay

On the northwest side of the island, just north of the Vesuvius ferry terminal. Mainly transient boaters, and busy in summer. A licensed restaurant with take-out and a coffee shop are within walking distance, and short hiking trails start from the parking lot.

From October 18, BC Ferries operates water taxis on the south side of the dock.

  • Honour pay box
  • Wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • Night lighting
  • Bus service
  • Parking
04Burgoyne Bay1 dock

Burgoyne Bay

On the west side, at the north end of Sansum Narrows across from Maple Bay. The small bay is sheltered from south and east wind and waves.

Half the float is reserved for the Burgoyne Bay Residents Association (yellow tie-up rail). The other half is transient use, up to 48 hours.

  • Honour pay box
  • Wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • Solar beacon at night
  • Some parking
05Musgrave Landing1 dock

Musgrave Landing

On the west side, at the south end of Sansum Narrows across from Cowichan Bay. Mainly transient boaters, and busy in summer.

The road is unsurfaced and can be hard for low-clearance vehicles in bad weather.

  • Honour pay box
  • Wharfinger visits
  • No services
  • A few parking spots

In Ganges, 30-amp power, water, Wi-Fi, showers and garbage disposal are offered for customers paying moorage on vessels over 12 feet. Canada Border Services is available in Ganges by prior arrangement: call 1-888-226-7277.

Moorage estimator

Transient moorage at the published rates.

$0

    An estimate only. The Harbour Office confirms the final charge. Transient stays are limited to 72 hours unless the Harbour Manager authorizes more.

    Day visits

    From 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, two hours of moorage are free. After 2 hours, half the daily rate applies. After 4:00 PM, the full daily rate applies. Check-out is 10:00 AM.

    Zero discharge

    Ganges is a zero-discharge harbour. Vessels with holding tanks are asked to use the pump-out at the Breakwater Float. Water is free with paid moorage, with no wash-downs, and is turned off in winter.

    Power

    30-amp connections are at Centennial, Kanaka and the Breakwater. Proper twist-lock marine connectors are required. No pig tails.

    Centennial reserves

    Dinghy float $61.90 per month plus tax. Small boat floats $21.00 per month plus tax, charged quarterly, plus moorage. First float commercial $525.00 per year plus tax, plus moorage.

    03 · Long-term moorage

    Get on the list.

    5+ yrs

    Applications are being accepted. Numbers on file already cover more than 5 years at the current turnover rate. Salt Spring Island residents get top priority.

    Monthly and quarterly moorage are offered at Centennial and Fulford Inner year round, and at Kanaka from October 1 to April 30. If you have an application on file, check in from time to time to keep it current.

    04 · Fish sales

    Straight off the boat.

    The Harbour Authority manages a fish sales dock at Centennial Dock, by the harbour office at 127 Fulford-Ganges Road. Commercial fishers sell direct to the public.

    As much advance notice as possible is given, and signs go up when sales happen.

    • Shrimp
    • Tuna
    • Salmon
    • Crab

    06 · Notices

    On the water now.

    • OCT 18

      Vesuvius water taxis

      BC Ferries is operating water taxis on the south side of the Vesuvius dock.

    • OCT 1

      Winter moorage

      Winter moorage runs October 1 to May 1 on the inside of Kanaka Dock. Transient moorage stays open on the outside of the Kanaka header float and on the Breakwater Dock. Keep clear of the yellow float plane areas.

    • JUN 18

      Pump-out open

      The pump-out is open again after repairs.

    • JUN 5

      New rates

      Transient moorage is $1.60 per foot per night on all Salt Spring Island docks, and 30-amp power is $10.00 per night.

    • MAY 18

      Float planes

      Harbour Air moors float planes on the centre finger of the Breakwater Dock. They back up and turn between Breakwater and Kanaka, so keep a lookout.

    • APR 30

      Used oil

      Used motor oil is accepted from vessels only. Containers are not accepted.

    07 · Membership

    Locally run.

    HASSI is a not-for-profit that operates, maintains and manages public fishing harbours, providing facilities and services to community and marine stakeholders. To take part in running it, join the Harbour Authority of Salt Spring Island Association. The Board of Directors approves membership, and applications are at the main harbour office.

    1. 1990

      Incorporated by Letters Patent on May 23, 1990. First licence covers Burgoyne Bay, Fulford Inner, Ganges Centennial, Ganges Kanaka, Musgrave Landing and Vesuvius Bay.

    2. 1997

      January 9: the former transport dock at Fulford Outer Harbour is added.

    3. 1998

      April 27: the former transport dock in Ganges, the Coast Guard wharf, is added. Eight facilities in five harbours.
