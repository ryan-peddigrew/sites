about

Cielle & Co is built to scale vision, accelerate momentum, and deliver world-class experiences.

Led by Carly Silberstein, a proven entrepreneur and industry force, Cielle & Co exists for one purpose: to bring big ideas to life. From event design to strategic consulting, we operate at the intersection of business acumen and creative precision. Founder-led but built to scale, we partner with brands, entrepreneurs, and organizations that are ready to grow—and unwilling to settle for ordinary.

From startup to scale, this isn't our first chapter—it's the next one.

our story

Cielle & Co was born from a decade of entrepreneurial leadership, creative problem solving, and doing things differently. Founded by Carly Silberstein after growing one of Canada's leading event agencies, Cielle & Co represents the next evolution in how event services are delivered: bold, boutique, strategic, and scalable.

We're not here to follow tradition. We're here to build what's next—with intention, edge, and excellence.

good questions Added

Before we talk.

Do you take on single events or ongoing work?

Both. Some clients bring us one flagship moment. Others keep us on as a fractional events team or an executive seat at the table.

When should we reach out?

Earlier than you think. Strategy has the most leverage before venues, dates and budgets are locked, but we've joined mid-stream plenty of times.

Do you work alongside in-house teams?

Yes. We slot in beside marketing, sales and leadership teams, and we're comfortable leading, supporting or advising.

Where do you work?

Based in Toronto and built to travel. Wherever the event is, so are we.

What does fractional executive leadership look like?

A senior events leader embedded in your business for a set number of days a month, without the full-time hire.
