What we do

Since 1998, Heller Productions has managed corporate and non-profit events, retail conferences, golf tournaments and incentive travel trips, just to name a few.

We have raised the bar in the events industry around and beyond the Greater Toronto Area, and gained a reputation as one of Canada’s finest boutique event companies.
