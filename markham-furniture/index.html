How it's made

The gold standard in seating.

  1. Canadian kiln-dried hardwood framesSolid wood frames built from locally sourced Canadian hardwood.
  2. Eight-way hand-tied coil springsEach coil is tied and knotted by hand in eight directions with twine.
  3. Reinforced seating and cornersEvery corner is double dowelled, glued, blocked and screwed together.
  4. Premium cushioningHigh resiliency foam wrapped with dacron fibre keeps its shape over time.
  5. Tailored to perfectionYour fabric is cut, sewn and applied by upholsterers with decades of experience.

Our story

One man's bench.
Since 1998.

After more than two decades in fine upholstery manufacturing at high-end furniture plants in Ontario, William "Bill" Rizk set out to build his own line of fine upholstered furniture. He started in a small shop on Denison Street in Markham.

Today a team of carpenters, cutters, sewers and upholsterers builds every piece to order. Family owned and operated. Nine-time Readers' Choice Award winner.
