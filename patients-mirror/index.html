Who we are, and why it matters

We are skilled storytellers and attentive listeners. But we are also just like you: patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals. And we too have a story.

We are a professional group specializing in marketing and documentary production. We bring narratives to life with passion and precision.

We are driven to make a positive impact for the family and friends we have lost, for the patients whose stories touch our hearts, for forgotten seniors, for people trying to navigate the healthcare system, and for the healthcare workers who serve despite burnout. Together, we are making a difference.

What we do

Together with our partners, we create.

Patients at the forefront: their journeys, challenges and triumphs. It gives a voice to the people directly affected and builds empathy and understanding in everyone who watches.

Caregivers play a vital role. Our films show their dedication and the emotional and physical challenges they face, validate other caregivers, and raise awareness of the support they need.

Films that bridge the gap between patients and the people who care for them. They demystify medical processes and treatments and show the innovations and challenges in healthcare.

Rare disease, mental health, everyday wellness, breakthrough therapies or medical devices: every film is made to inform, so viewers can make better health choices.

It's not just about making the film. It's about impact: sparking discussion and showing decision makers the real-world effect of healthcare policy on patients and providers.

The latest documentary filmmaking technology, so every production is both informative and visually captivating, and reaches a wider audience.

Share your story

Your privacy comes first,
at every step.

Thinking about taking part in a documentary? Here's exactly what happens. You stay in control, and you can stop at any point.

  1. Tell us you're interested

    Just your name and phone number. Please don't share any medical details yet.

  2. A first phone conversation

    We call you to understand your interest and see whether it's the right fit.

  3. Consent, in writing

    If we both want to go ahead, you complete a patient information consent release form that protects your privacy and rights.

  4. A full interview

    A detailed online interview about life with your condition: the highs, the lows and your hopes.

  5. Your information, removed if we stop

    If either of us decides not to go ahead, all your contact information is removed from our records.
