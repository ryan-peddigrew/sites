Competitors, elevation and revenue

Where Patients' Mirror wins.

Patients' Mirror makes documentaries that put patients at the centre of healthcare, funded by sponsors. It does not sell general video production; that's what keeps it distinct. The new site leans hard into that, and turns sponsorship into a guided path instead of a contact form.

  • The gap: most rivals are either agencies selling video to pharma, or nonprofits and hospitals making films in-house. Few in Canada offer sponsor-funded, patient-first documentaries with a clear way to buy in. That's Patients' Mirror's lane.
  • What they had that others don't: a written patient privacy process, Canadian focus (including Quebec drug plans), and a team with marketing and documentary production.
How we elevated it

Five big moves.

  • One clear story: "No patient should be ignored." replaces four mixed hero slides.
  • Films first: their own films now play on the page, so sponsors judge the work in seconds.
  • Three audiences, three doors: patients, sponsors and healthcare professionals each get a path.
  • Trust built in: their privacy steps are shown up front and the form only asks for name and phone, as their own rules say.
  • Refreshed logo and look: same mark, cleaner name, dark "screening room" palette from their navy and green.
Revenue

Capturing sponsors faster.

The old site said "contact us". The new one scopes the deal before the first call.

Built

Sponsorship brief builder

Six taps: who you are, how you want to partner (their three options), focus, goals, add-ons and timing. Their team gets a ready brief and can reply with options the same day.

Built

Add-ons inside the brief

Social cut-downs, premiere screening, panel discussion, professional education version, impact campaign and French version. Each raises the size of a sponsorship. Their sponsorship page already mentions screenings, panels and campaigns.

Built

More patients coming forward

A short, safe form and clear steps mean more stories to film, and more projects to offer sponsors.
