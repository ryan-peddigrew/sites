    See what the eye can't

    Water spreads behind walls and under floors before it shows. A thermal camera picks up the cold, wet patches so we know where to look.

    Thermal imagingFinding the source
  2. Step 2 · Measure it

    Put a number on wet

    A moisture meter confirms what the camera shows, so drying is based on readings, not guesses.

  3. Step 3 · Open it up

    What was hiding

    Pulling the baseboard showed mould starting behind it. Damaged material comes out so the space underneath can dry properly.

    Baseboard removedMould found
  4. Step 4 · Dry it out

    Air movers and a dehumidifier set up through the unit, from the living area down the hall.
