Brand style guide · version 1 · November 2026

MapleTrust, in one page.

How the logo, colours, type and buttons work, so the website, trucks, uniforms, invoices and social posts all look like the same trusted team.

01 · Logo

The leaf that shelters a home.

A maple leaf (Canadian, local) holding a small house under a navy roof line (we protect your place). The name sits beside it: "Maple" in navy, "Trust" in maple red, "RESTORATION" spaced out underneath.

MapleTrustRESTORATION
Main logo, on light backgrounds
MapleTrustRESTORATION
On navy: white name, brighter red "Trust"
App icon, social profile picture, favicon

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the house.
  • Use the mark on its own only where space is tight (icon, stamp, truck door).
  • Keep the logo at least 120px wide on screen, 30mm in print.

Don't

  • Change the colours, stretch, rotate or outline it.
  • Add shadows, glows or a reflection.
  • Put it on a busy photo without a solid panel behind it.
02 · Colour

Three working colours, plus neutrals.

Navy#0F2440

Headings, header bar, footer. The colour of trust.

Maple red#D62B1F

Call buttons, the leaf, "Trust". Use for action, sparingly.

Water blue#2F7DE1

Accents only: the wave line, water in illustrations.

Paper#FBF8F3

Page background. Warm, clean, calm.

Stone#F1EBE1

Panels and quieter sections.

Slate#55607A

Body text and captions.

Bright red#FF4A3A

Red on navy only, so it stays readable.

White#FFFFFF

Cards, text on navy and red.

03 · Type

Bold headlines, easy reading.

Water, fire or mould?

Bricolage Grotesque · ExtraBold 800 · tight spacing · headlines and the logo name

Expert restoration in Toronto and the GTA, ensuring your property is safely restored with care. When disaster strikes, let us take the weight off your shoulders.

Instrument Sans · Regular 400 and SemiBold 600 · body text, buttons, forms

Both are free Google Fonts. For Word, email and invoices, use Arial if they aren't installed.

04 · Buttons and details

Clear, rounded, never round.

Call now 647-267-6798Send to MapleTrustTell us what happened

10px corners · at least 54px tall · red = call, navy = send, outline = second choice

The water line: one blue wave under a key word. Once per page.

Section label

Short red bar plus spaced capitals, above each section title.

Line icons, 40px, navy on light and white on navy. Never inside a box or circle.

Photos

Use real MapleTrust job photos first: meters, thermal cameras, air movers, finished rooms. Natural light, no heavy filters. Never show a customer's face or address without permission.

05 · Voice

Calm, quick, plain words.

Sounds like

  • "Water, fire or mould? We've got it."
  • "Tell us what happened."
  • "We answer 24/7."

Avoid

  • Jargon like "solutions" and "remediation services provider".
  • All caps sentences.
  • Promises we can't back up (times, guarantees) unless confirmed.
