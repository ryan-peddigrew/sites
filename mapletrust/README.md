## Design picks
- Logo A, "Leaf shelter": maple leaf holding a house under a navy roof line (img/mark.svg, mark-dark.svg, icon.svg).
- Light paper theme: navy #0F2440, maple red #D62B1F, water blue #2F7DE1, paper #FBF8F3, stone #F1EBE1.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Instrument Sans (body). 10px button corners, never round.
- Animated hero: illustrated room cycles Respond, Inspect (thermal scan), Dry out (air movers), Restore.
- Live "Open now" Toronto clock, service tiles with detail panels, real job photo wall, "Tell us what happened" form, ?tour=1 tour.
