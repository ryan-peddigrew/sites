Questions

Planner questions, answered.

Owners only: answers to confirm with MAC.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Send a quote request any time; busy season fills first.

Do you deliver, set up and run the show?

Yes. Delivery, setup, show technicians and strike can all be added to your quote.

Can I see the gear in my room first?

Use the floor planner to lay out your room, and our team will check it against the venue.

Do I need power or rigging information?

Send what you have from the venue. We'll confirm power and rigging before load-in.

Can I get a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Request one below and we'll send it for your venue.

Do you work outside Toronto?

Yes. We handle national rentals and tours.
