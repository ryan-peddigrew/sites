Install

Put an app on a phone or desktop

  • iPhone: open the app link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
  • Desktop: open it in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon at the right of the address bar. On a Mac in Safari: File, then Add to Dock.
