04 / Exhaust accessories

Flange to rain cap

A single source for every exhaust accessory. Gaskets and hardware come in kits to make ordering easy.

Engine flanges

A tight, reliable seal between engine and exhaust. Many thicknesses and materials.

Elbows

Short radius, long radius and multi-piece, to change direction without hurting flow.

Gaskets & hardware

Sealing and fastening for leak-proof connections, available in kits.

Y-connectors

Merge or split exhaust flow paths for better routing.

Flexes

Absorb movement and vibration to reduce stress on components.

Bellows

Take up thermal expansion and contraction to protect the system.

Stacks

Vertical exhaust pipes for generator and industrial applications.

Rain caps

Keep water out while letting exhaust gases escape.

Thimbles

Reinforced sections where pipes pass through walls or barriers.

Mounting bands

Clamps and bands that securely support exhaust components.

07 / Our story

Two people in Markham to two countries

Lean manufacturing since 1992

  1. 1992

    Jim Williams and his partners found E.I. Williams in Markham, Ontario, with two employees.

  2. 1999

    Outgrows the first location and moves to Ajax, Ontario: 7,000 square feet and five employees.

  3. 2006

    A new site in Ajax grows production and office space to 21,000 square feet, with new steel forming and welding equipment.

  4. 2012

    Larger shears, brake presses and roll-form equipment.

  5. 2013

    A new paint booth for higher volumes and larger silencers.

  6. 2016

    New production processes and a team of 15.

  7. 2019

    Expansion into the U.S. with a newly built facility in Sarepta, Louisiana: 50,000 square feet, with room for 50,000 more.

  8. 2020

    Ajax grows past 20 employees, and the Louisiana plant is running by December.

  9. 2023

    Investment in research and new emission products, including 3WCs, DOCs and DPFs.

  10. 2024

    Partnership with IMS to bring SCR solutions to North America.
