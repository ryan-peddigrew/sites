About WasteMind

About WasteMind

A community-centric waste management equipment company, focused on giving our customers viable and sustainable solutions.

Who we are

Industry experts in waste control and recycling optimization

WasteMind Building Solutions develops multi-tiered approaches to waste control and recycling optimization for condos, apartments, retirement residences and select commercial properties. We manufacture and maintain automated recycling systems, which makes us one of the most comprehensive and professional companies in North America.

We take great pride in our service. Our technicians have more than ten years of experience in the field, explain the work before they do it, and back it with 24-hour service. Our compactors and tri-sorters are built in our own Toronto facility, so the people who service your equipment are the same team that designed it.

From our Ottawa head office and our GTA representatives, we serve buildings from Ottawa and Eastern Ontario through Toronto to the Niagara region.

Our vision

An innovative, community-centric waste management company

To be an innovative community-centric waste management company with a focus on catering to our customers with viable and sustainable solutions.

Our mission

Proactive service, dependable machinery, a seasoned team

Collaborating with our clients, we aim to surpass their requirements for waste and recycling equipment by delivering proactive service, dependable machinery, and the support of North America's most seasoned team.

How we work

Four habits that stop repeat problems

  1. Diagnose first. We find the actual cause (electrical, mechanical or layout) before recommending a fix.
  2. Explain before we act. You get a clear explanation and options before any work is performed.
  3. Build to fit. Equipment is manufactured to your garbage room, not the other way around.
  4. Prevent the repeat. Preventative maintenance plans keep the problem from coming back.

Community

Part of the communities we serve

Being community-centric is more than a line in our vision statement. From sponsoring local events to lacing up as the "WasteMind Trashers" at the Stantec hockey tournament, we show up for the places where our customers live and work.
