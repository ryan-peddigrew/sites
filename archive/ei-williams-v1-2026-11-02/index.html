Who we are

We design and manufacture industrial exhaust systems, components and accessories. One source for every part of your exhaust.

Our story

From two people in Markham to two countries

1992

Jim Williams and his partners found E.I. Williams in Markham, Ontario, with two employees.

1999

Outgrows its first home and moves to Ajax, Ontario: 7,000 square feet and five employees.

2006

A new Ajax site grows production and office space to 21,000 square feet, with new steel forming and welding equipment.

2012

Larger shears, brake presses and roll-form equipment.

2013

A new paint booth for higher volumes and larger silencers.

2016

New production processes and a team of 15.

2019

Expansion into the U.S. with a newly built facility in Sarepta, Louisiana: 50,000 square feet, with room for 50,000 more.

2020

Ajax grows past 20 employees, and the Louisiana plant is running by December.

2023

Investment in research and new emission products, including 3WCs, DOCs and DPFs.

2024

Partnership with IMS to bring SCR solutions to North America.
