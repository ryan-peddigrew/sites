Each keynote runs 45 to 60 minutes, in person or virtual, and is customized to the audience and the event vision.

Building and Championing a Winning Brand

A strong brand helps you rise above the noise and the competition. But if the value you offer, the people you serve, and the problems you solve are not clear, your audience cannot connect with it.

This interactive session uses exercises to clarify your buyer persona, sharpen your value proposition, line up brand identity with brand perception, and communicate your message with impact.

Speak up when it counts

Do you wish you spoke up more in meetings, in boardrooms, at networking events, or in your annual review? Do you wish you had the courage to disagree out loud? Do you want to feel seen and heard?

Many people leave jobs, relationships, ventures, and conversations feeling they would have achieved more if they had shared feedback, ideas, or their value. This keynote digs into what holds people back, builds confidence for uncomfortable moments, and builds a culture of positive outspokenness.

45-60 minutesLeadership teamsMid-level managersWomenSmall business ownersEntrepreneursAssociationsCorporationsNonprofits

Changing Culture, Building Trust, and Cultivating Leaders

When employees, customers, and stakeholders walk out the door, companies lose a great deal each year. It is time to focus on what matters most: your people.

Empathy, emotional intelligence, and servant leadership carry new weight as work keeps changing. This keynote covers simple, powerful shifts that humanize interactions, improve morale, and build a culture based on courage and trust.

About

Not just another keynote speaker

Courtney Stanley is an award-winning changemaker, keynote speaker, event emcee, and creator of the women-inspired podcast Dare to Interrupt.

Through her leadership and use of voice, she has earned seats on prestigious international boards of directors as the youngest member ever inducted.

She has spent 10 years helping women, young professionals, entrepreneurs, and organizations engage in game-changing, impactful conversations that help people tap into their true potential, improve team and culture dynamics, and drive meaningful change.

She holds a degree from Central Michigan University in experience design, leadership studies, and business hospitality.

She is personally most proud of her work to prevent sexual harassment and assault through the launch of an events-centered social movement, #MeetingsToo.

Her mission is to empower women and ambitious professionals through vulnerable, meaningful dialogue, leading to changed behaviors and new approaches to leadership on stages and in boardrooms across North America and internationally.
