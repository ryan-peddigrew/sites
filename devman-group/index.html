How a refurbishment runs.

Every project is given an on-site manager. Our job is to minimize disruption for residents and keep management informed from start to finish.

  1. Step 1Site meeting

    We walk the building with your management team and listen to what residents need.

  2. Step 2Value-engineer the budget

    We work with you to value-engineer the scope and prepare a budget.

  3. Step 3Design with our partners

    Our partner design firms create a design custom to your building's vision and demographic.

  4. Step 4Build with an on-site manager

    Scheduling, updates and site reviews, with honest, upfront timelines.

  5. Step 5Final installation

    Timeless design and superior installations, with meticulous attention to detail.
